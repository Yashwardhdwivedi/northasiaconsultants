Straight answers

Questions worth asking

Will you get me a job in the Gulf?

No, and anyone who says they will for a fee is not telling you the truth. We prepare your application so that when you apply, you're competitive — a CV in the format Gulf operators read, a clear picture of which certifications your trade needs, and coaching for the trade test and interview. Where you apply and what comes of it is yours.

Do you send my CV to employers?

No. We give you a briefed list of where to apply — operator career portals, drilling contractor sites, licensed agencies in your region — and you apply directly. That keeps you in control and means nobody is charging you for access to a vacancy.

What if I'm not happy with the CV?

We revise it until you are. There's no revision limit and no time limit on getting it right.

How do I know an agency is legitimate?

Check their Registration Certificate on the eMigrate portal, insist on a receipt for anything you pay, and refuse to pay before you hold a written employment offer. Those three habits filter out most of what goes wrong.

Do I need BOSIET and HUET before applying?

Depends entirely on your trade and whether the work is offshore. Some candidates spend ₹40,000 on tickets they didn't need for the roles they're targeting. Part of the package is telling you honestly which ones matter for you.

Is the ₹4,999 refundable?

Before we've started work, yes — message us on WhatsApp. Once your CV is drafted and delivered, the work has been done and the fee stands.
